# ExportIQ Vision

ExportIQ is an AI-powered Global Trade Intelligence Platform.

Its purpose is to help exporters discover, understand and engage with buyers around the world.

ExportIQ is not a CRM.

ExportIQ is not an ERP.

ExportIQ is a continuously evolving knowledge graph of global trade organisations, products, people and relationships.

Every fact in ExportIQ should be traceable to one or more sources.

AI should enhance knowledge but never overwrite verified facts.
